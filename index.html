How a week works
  • Every action uses 1 or 2 of the day's 12 blocks. A block is two hours.
  • Food, Water, Energy and Social fall every block. If any of them hits 0, Carlos is done.
  • Rent comes out at the end of every 7th day. If Carlos cannot cover it, he is evicted. Watch the rent bar under his meters.
  • The theater pays cash for a two-block shift. Shifts start between 12p and 8p, and the pay rises as he gets promoted.
  • Chilling on Discord with the boys fills Social. Low Social cuts training XP, and a high Stank makes Social drain faster.
  • The shop sells upgrades for meals, water, sleep, Discord, Smash, shifts and training. Buying is instant and permanent for this Carlos.
  • Tournaments run a real bracket against three opponents. Win all three for the big cash prize. Skill and a fight stick raise the odds.
  • Random events can be good, bad, or his friends. Bad luck is real, but Stank makes it less likely.
  • Night runs from 10p to 6a. Sleep restores more then, and everything else costs extra energy. Two or more night sleeps give +25% training XP the next day.
