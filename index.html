How a week works
  • Every new Carlos starts with a trait you pick. Each one has an upside and a downside.
  • Every action uses 1 or 2 of the day's 12 blocks. A block is two hours.
  • Food, Water, Energy and Social fall every block. If any of them hits 0, Carlos is done.
  • Rent is charged for each day at the rate of wherever Carlos lives, and the week's total comes out at the end of every 7th day. If he cannot cover it, he is evicted.
  • A theater shift is a 15-second ticket-tearing game. A great shift pays more and fills the promotion bar faster. A rough one pays less and pulls the bar back. Shifts start between 12p and 8p.
  • Chilling on Discord with the boys fills Social. Low Social cuts training XP.
  • The Shop button in the corner sells upgrades and lets Carlos move. Bigger homes cost more rent, but his needs drain slower, sleep is better and bad luck is rarer.
  • Tournaments are a three-round arrow rhythm game. A higher Smash level and a fight stick make notes slower, timing looser and targets lower. Win all three rounds for $150 and a trophy. There is one tournament a week.
  • Teach Gus tricks to level him up. A smarter Gus causes less trouble and brings much better surprises.
  • Random events can be good, bad, his friends, or Gus, his cat. Bad luck is real, but Stank makes it less likely.
  • Once a month (every 28 days) a hard event hits: a surprise inspection, the flu, a kickboxing match, Dustin's album and more. Carlos gets a few days to finish the goals. Beat it for a big reward, or eat a big penalty.
  • To win, live in Carlos Manor for 28 days in total and own every shop upgrade.
  • Night runs from 10p to 6a. Sleep restores more then, and everything else costs extra energy. Two or more night sleeps give +25% training XP the next day.
